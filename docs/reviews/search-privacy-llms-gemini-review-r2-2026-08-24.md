### NOTE
* **PDPA Article 3 Statutory Alignment**: The revised English and Traditional Chinese sentences accurately map to all five rights set forth in Article 3 of Taiwan’s Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA):
  1. Inquiry/Review (*查詢或閱覽* / *access or review*)
  2. Request a copy (*取得複製本 / 製給複製本* / *obtain a copy*)
  3. Supplement or correction (*補充或更正* / *supplement or correct*)
  4. Cessation of collection, processing, or use (*停止蒐集、處理或利用* / *stop its collection, processing, or use*)
  5. Erasure (*刪除* / *delete*)

  The phrasing remains natural, clear, and fully coordinated across both language editions while resolving the prior omissions (supplementation and collection/processing cessation).
