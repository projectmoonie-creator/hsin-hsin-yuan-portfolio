# Overclocking Card Reel

## Concept angle

Engineering ideas become physical through hands, tools, mechanisms, and tests.
The remembered signature is the improbable machine working in the real world,
not an added technology graphic.

## Composition

- Full-bleed 16:9 footage with the source crop preserved.
- The prototype or mechanism remains the focal element in every beat.
- Workshop edges, hands, and test environments provide supporting detail.
- The source image supplies all foreground, midground, and background depth.
- No portfolio-added text, decorative overlays, or artificial camera motion.

## Treatment

- Preserve source color and archival texture.
- Remove source audio.
- Use only short crossfades to soften compression-era shot changes.
- Poster: a clean green-frame and chain-drive assembly close-up, without a
  group-photo composition.

## Rhythm

`MECHANISM-build-PROPELLER-assembly-TEST-detail`

The first four beats establish process and the final two deliver action and a
precise mechanical close.
