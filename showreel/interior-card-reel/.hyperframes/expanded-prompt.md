# Interior Card Reel - Expanded Prompt

Create a 14-second, 1280 x 720, 30 fps silent moving thumbnail from confirmed
Interior / Spatial Brand Films work directed and edited by Hsin-Hsin Yuan.

The reel appears beneath a portfolio card's existing title and metadata. Do not
add typography, captions, UI, a logo lockup, an end slate, narration, music, or
source audio. Footage alone carries the reel.

Use ten compact full-bleed shots that each add a distinct function: room scale,
circulation, material, cooking, lived use, framed art, product integration,
television-as-object, tactile detail, and a final room hold. Favor hard cuts and
visible picture movement. Keep faces and burned-in source graphics away from the
card's outer edges where possible because the website crops the 16:9 video into
a 16:10 card.

Apply a restrained editorial grade only: saturation around 92%, contrast around
105%, and brightness around 95%. Do not imitate footage, invent client work, or
merge this reel with PTS Taigi material.
