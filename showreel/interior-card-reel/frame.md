# Interior / Spatial Brand Films - Card Reel

## Purpose

A 14-second, silent, 16:9 moving thumbnail for the Interior / Spatial Brand
Films portfolio card. The site supplies the title, platform, role, and tagline,
so the video itself contains no text or logos added by the portfolio.

## Visual Identity

- Full-bleed authored footage
- Neutral editorial grade: slightly restrained saturation, clear blacks
- Hard cuts driven by motion and visual function
- No end card, no transition graphics, no source audio
- Avoid host-led shots when an architectural, material, human-use, or product
  image communicates the work more directly

## Rhythm

`SPACE-space-MATERIAL-human-use-BRAND-detail-ROOM`

The loop should feel useful at small size and remain readable when center-cropped
inside a 16:10 card.
