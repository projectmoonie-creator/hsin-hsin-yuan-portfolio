# Interior + PTS Portfolio Reel - Expanded Production Prompt

## Style Block

Create a 54-second, 1920 x 1080, 30 fps website-first portfolio reel for
Hsin-Hsin Yuan. The visual world treats architectural sightlines and Taiwanese
bus routes as one continuous act of observation: stories begin inside designed
spaces, then travel outward through roads, places, and people.

Use the exact website palette:

- Background `#0B0B0C`
- Foreground `#F7F2E8`
- Muted text `#B8B0A3`
- Primary accent `#D8FF3E`
- Secondary edit punctuation `#FF4D1F`

Use `League Gothic` for large display type, `Montserrat` for editorial body
copy, and `JetBrains Mono` for metadata and credit labels.

The mood is cinematic editorial observation: precise but warm, international
without becoming anonymous, and useful as a resume supplement without reading
like an agency advertisement.

## Rhythm

`cold-open-BUILD-human-hold-brand-PIVOT-local-breathe-END`

The edit rises quickly through space and material, becomes warmer through
human use, peaks on transformation and brand integration, then pivots into a
shorter local-culture chapter before a quiet end card.

## Global Rules

- Footage is the primary focal point in every footage scene.
- Keep at least two focal points: the moving image and one restrained editorial
  detail such as a credit label, route line, or registration mark.
- Maintain a recurring acid-green route line across chapters. It DRAWS,
  TRACES, TURNS, and LOCKS to meaningful architectural or geographic edges.
- Use full-bleed or split-frame footage, never a grid of identical UI cards.
- Graphics frame the footage; they do not cover important faces, rooms, or products.
- Use hard cuts and match cuts for rapid footage passages.
- Use one directional wipe for the Interior-to-PTS pivot.
- Ambient graphics BREATH slowly; do not add generic glow fields, glitch,
  liquid distortion, or decorative gradients.
- Do not reuse source music in the public master unless it is cleared.
- Interior chapter credit: `DIRECTOR + EDITOR`.
- PTS chapter credit: `PLANNING + SCRIPT`.

## Scene 1 - Cold Open

**Time:** `00:00-00:03.2`

**Concept:** The viewer arrives inside a spatial decision already in motion.
Three or four visual fragments CUT through light, greenery, a room reveal, and
a human hand. The name appears only after the footage earns attention.

**Mood:** Contemporary documentary title sequence; architectural magazine
precision with the tactile timing of a film editor's reel.

**Depth:**

- BG: full-bleed footage; subtle neutral grain; one oversized ghost slash.
- MG: name and `DIRECTOR + EDITOR`.
- FG: route-line segment, top-right frame count, two registration corners.

**Choreography:**

- Footage CUTS on picture movement.
- Route line DRAWS from an architectural edge.
- Name SLIDES in from the left and LOCKS to the line.
- Credit TYPES ON in JetBrains Mono.
- Registration marks PULSE once, then settle.

**Transition:** Hard cut on matched lateral motion.

## Scene 2 - Space / Light / Material

**Time:** `00:03.2-00:14.0`

**Concept:** Rooms are read as living systems rather than static property
images. Wide movement gives way to light, texture, circulation, and material
detail.

**Mood:** Slow architectural cinema with clean magazine typography.

**Depth:**

- BG: alternating full-bleed and 70/30 split footage; subtle grain.
- MG: `SPACE / LIGHT / MATERIAL`.
- FG: route line, source index, small directional ticks, one warm-white rule.

**Choreography:**

- Wide shots PUSH horizontally.
- Detail shots CUT closer on matching geometry.
- Chapter words APPEAR one at a time and HOLD.
- Route line TRACES corners and circulation paths.
- Source index STEPS upward with each source change.

**Transition:** Focus-pull crossfade, `0.35s`, `power2.inOut`, into human action.

## Scene 3 - Designed for Living

**Time:** `00:14.0-00:25.0`

**Concept:** Architecture becomes meaningful through use. Cooking, pets, hands,
homeowners, and designers turn polished rooms into lived environments.

**Mood:** Warm observational documentary; intimate without sentimentality.

**Depth:**

- BG: full-bleed human detail and domestic action.
- MG: `DESIGNED FOR LIVING`.
- FG: quiet chapter label, two hairline dividers, minimal timecode metadata.

**Choreography:**

- Human gestures CUT on action.
- One longer shot BREATHES for `2.5-3.2s`.
- Chapter title FLOATS up softly, then recedes.
- Hairlines GROW from the gesture direction.
- Metadata CLICKS to the opposite edge from faces.

**Transition:** Match cut from a hand or moving object into product use.

## Scene 4 - Story / Function / Desire

**Time:** `00:25.0-00:37.5`

**Concept:** The reel shows commercial fluency without becoming an
advertisement. Renovation logic and product integration are framed as parts of
real life: a transformed room, a television within an art context, an
appliance within cooking, and a branded home within daily use.

**Mood:** Premium factual brand film; exact, confident, and human.

**Depth:**

- BG: transformation and brand footage.
- MG: `STORY / FUNCTION / DESIRE`.
- FG: partner metadata, route line, one heat-orange edit mark, registration corners.

**Choreography:**

- Before/after shots SNAP through shared geometry.
- Product reveals PUSH in, then HOLD.
- Partner metadata TYPES ON without becoming a logo wall.
- Heat-orange mark FLASHES once at the sequence peak.
- Route line EXPANDS toward the right edge.

**Transition:** The route line WIPES right in `0.45s`, becoming a road trace.

## Scene 5 - Taiwan / On the Road

**Time:** `00:37.5-00:39.5`

**Concept:** A spatial line exits the home and becomes a route. This is the
single formal bridge between two different bodies of work.

**Mood:** Graphic but understated public-media interstitial.

**Depth:**

- BG: black field with a faint source-image afterglow.
- MG: acid route line and `TAIWAN / ON THE ROAD`.
- FG: three location ticks and a small `PTS TAIGI` identifier.

**Choreography:**

- Route line TURNS and TRACES across the frame.
- Location ticks CLICK into place.
- Label TYPES ON.
- Afterglow BREATHES once.

**Transition:** Directional wipe follows the route line into bus movement.

## Scene 6 - PTS Taigi / Nothing by Bus

**Time:** `00:39.5-00:49.5`

**Concept:** The pace opens into Taiwanese roads, markets, food, craft, coast,
and local faces. The chapter demonstrates editorial and script work, so the
credit is explicit and persistent enough to prevent authorship ambiguity.

**Mood:** Public-media travel factual; observant, local, and curious.

**Depth:**

- BG: bus, road, coast, market, food, craft, and people.
- MG: `NOTHING BY BUS`.
- FG: persistent `PLANNING + SCRIPT`, episode/location metadata, route line.

**Choreography:**

- Road and bus shots PUSH forward.
- Place details CUT in compact two- or three-shot phrases.
- `PTS TAIGI / 2021` and the program title SLIDE in and HOLD.
- `PLANNING + SCRIPT` LOCKS beneath the title for at least `3s`.
- Location metadata TYPES ON at each episode change.
- Route line DRIFTS between location ticks.

**Transition:** Gentle blur crossfade, `0.5s`, into the end card.

## Scene 7 - End Card

**Time:** `00:49.5-00:54.0`

**Concept:** The reel ends with confidence and room to breathe. It names the
author, frames the body of work, and returns the viewer to the portfolio.

**Mood:** Quiet festival-program end slate, not a sales CTA.

**Depth:**

- BG: `#0B0B0C`, subtle grain, one source-image afterglow.
- MG: `HSIN-HSIN YUAN` and `SELECTED FACTUAL + BRANDED WORK`.
- FG: portfolio URL, route line, final frame count, registration corners.

**Choreography:**

- Name RISES from the route line.
- Descriptor FADES in.
- URL TYPES ON.
- Route line DRAWS to completion and HOLDS.
- All ambient elements settle for the final `1.5s`.

## Recurring Motifs

- One acid-green route line that changes meaning across chapters.
- JetBrains Mono source and role metadata.
- Warm-white structural rules aligned to real picture geometry.
- Occasional ghost slash or oversized chapter word at controlled opacity.
- One heat-orange punctuation at the Interior chapter peak only.

## Negative Prompt

- No generic motivational copy.
- No unverified performance statistics.
- No equal-weight card grids.
- No gradient text.
- No purple-blue neon palette.
- No constant glow field.
- No glitch, VHS, liquid distortion, or excessive shader transitions.
- No source title cards or platform bumpers unless essential for context.
- No ambiguous role wording.
- No footage generated to imitate work that was not actually produced.
