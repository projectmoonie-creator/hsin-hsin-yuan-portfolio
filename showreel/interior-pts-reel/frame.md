# Hsin-Hsin Yuan - Interior + PTS Portfolio Reel

## Concept

Architectural sightlines become bus routes. The camera first traces how people
inhabit designed spaces, then follows stories moving through Taiwan. The reel
should feel observant, warm, international, and editorial rather than like a
hard-sell agency montage.

## Format

- Landscape: 1920 x 1080
- Duration: 54 seconds
- Frame rate: 30 fps
- Website-first master
- No narration
- A later 9:16 derivative may be made from this master

## Visual Identity

- Background: `#0B0B0C`
- Foreground: `#F7F2E8`
- Muted text: `#B8B0A3`
- Primary accent: `#D8FF3E`
- Secondary accent: `#FF4D1F`, used only for one or two edit punctuation marks
- Display type: `League Gothic`
- Editorial body: `Montserrat`
- Metadata: `JetBrains Mono`

## Credit Rules

- Interior / Spatial Brand Films: `Director / Editor`
- PTS Taigi - `Nothing by Bus`: `Planning / Script`
- Credits must remain visible long enough to read.
- Do not merge the two credit lines into one ambiguous role list.
- Do not use unverified audience, award, or sales claims.

## Direction

- Footage remains the hero. Graphic overlays should frame it, not compete with it.
- Use one recurring acid-green route line to connect rooms, hands, products,
  roads, and places.
- Prefer hard cuts, match cuts, and restrained directional wipes.
- Avoid showy glitch, liquid distortion, neon fog, generic gradient text,
  testimonial copy, and stacked UI cards.
- Keep source logos incidental unless their display is needed for context.
- No reused source music in the public master unless separately cleared.

## Rhythm

`cold-open-BUILD-human-hold-brand-PIVOT-local-breathe-END`

1. Cold visual hook
2. Space, light, and material
3. People, use, and change
4. Brand integration in lived environments
5. Route-line pivot
6. PTS Taigi planning / script chapter
7. Quiet end card
