## Shot Selection Rules

1. Use a shot only when it adds a new visual function: movement, scale,
   material, human use, transformation, brand integration, or place.
2. Favor shots between `0.8s` and `2.2s`; allow two longer `2.5-3.2s` breaths.
3. Do not repeat the same room, person, or camera move merely to fill time.
4. Avoid source title cards, burned-in advertisements, platform bumpers, and
   long host-to-camera passages unless context requires them.
5. Crop conservatively. Protect faces, product marks, and architectural lines.
6. Record the exact source URL and in/out timecode for every selected shot.

## Audio Direction

- Mood: warm editorial pulse with organic percussion and restrained low-end.
- Target tempo: approximately `92-100 BPM`.
- No lyrics, cinematic trailer swells, corporate ukulele, or aggressive EDM.
- Let the PTS pivot briefly expose location ambience if clean and cleared.
- Mix for website playback: integrated loudness around `-16 LUFS`, true peak
  no higher than `-1 dBTP`.
