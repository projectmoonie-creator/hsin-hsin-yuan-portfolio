# Nothing by Bus - Card Reel

## Purpose

A 10-second, silent, 16:9 moving thumbnail for the Nothing by Bus portfolio
card. The site supplies the program title, PTS Taigi platform, year, and
Planning / Script credit.

## Visual Identity

- Full-bleed public-program footage
- Roads, buses, place, food, craft, and local detail
- No portfolio-added typography or graphics
- No source audio
- Avoid centering the female host; use the program's places and observed
  details to represent the editorial work

## Rhythm

`BUS-road-DETAIL-bus-FOOD-craft`

The loop should read quickly at card size while keeping the role boundary clear
through the surrounding site copy.
